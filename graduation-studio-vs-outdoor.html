01 · COMPARISON

两者比较Studio vs Outdoor at a Glance

影棚拍摄 STUDIO
可控的灯光与背景 Controlled lighting & backdrop
不受天气影响 Weather-proof
适合正式、干净的风格 Clean, formal look
拍摄时间较有效率 Efficient shoot time
户外拍摄 OUTDOOR
自然光线与真实场景 Natural light & real scenery

适合校园、地标等有意义的地点 Great for campus or landmark locations

03 · WHICH TO CHOOSE

怎么选择？Which Should You Choose?

如果你重视效率、干净的画面与稳定品质，影棚拍摄会是更省心的选择；如果你想要在校园或有纪念意义的地点留下真实的回忆，户外拍摄会更有故事性。也可以两者结合，兼顾专业感与真实感。If you value efficiency, clean visuals and consistent quality, a studio session is the easier choice. If you want real memories at your campus or a meaningful location, outdoor shooting adds more story. Combining both is also a great option.

04 · FINAL THOUGHTS

最后的建议Final Thoughts

没有绝对的对错，只有适不适合你。先想清楚你想要的风格与预算，再和摄影师讨论拍摄计划，确保双方对成品有一致的期待。There's no right or wrong answer — only what fits you best. Decide on your preferred style and budget first, then discuss the shoot plan with your photographer to align expectations.
