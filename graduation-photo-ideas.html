02 · STUDIO VS OUTDOOR

影棚正式风格 vs 校园户外轻松风格Studio Formal vs Campus Outdoor

影棚拍摄不受天气与时间影响，专业打光让学士服、流苏与证书都拍得清楚有质感，适合想要正式、干净背景的毕业照；校园户外拍摄则更有纪念意义，能拍下你熟悉的校园场景，风格也更轻松自然。两种都可以安排，也可以在同一次预约中都拍。Studio sessions are weather-proof with consistent professional lighting that makes your gown, tassel and certificate look sharp — ideal for a clean, formal look. Campus outdoor sessions capture the places you know from your student years, with a more relaxed, natural feel. Both are available, and you can even do both in one booking.

FAQ

毕业照可以拍哪些组合？What combinations can I shoot?
个人独照、毕业＋家人、毕业＋朋友、毕业＋伴侣、毕业＋宠物、证书／学位照、学士服写真，都是常见的拍摄组合。Individual portraits, graduation + family, + friends, + partner, + pet, diploma/certificate photos and gown portraits are all common options.
影棚拍摄和校园户外拍摄该怎么选？Studio or campus outdoor — how do I choose?
影棚拍摄不受天气影响，打光稳定；校园户外拍摄风格更轻松、更有纪念意义。两种都可以安排，也可以在同一次预约中都拍。Studio is weather-proof with consistent lighting; campus outdoor feels more personal and relaxed. You can book either, or both in one session.
一定要用毕业袍拍才正式吗？Do I need the gown for a formal look?
不一定，正式的学士服写真与轻松的私服风格都可以拍，依你想要的感觉选择。Not necessarily — both formal gown portraits and a relaxed casual style are available, depending on the mood you want.
