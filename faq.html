📸 PASSPORT & VISA PHOTOGRAPHY

护照 / 签证证件照Passport & Visa Photo

拍 Passport Photo 需要预约吗？Do I need to make a booking for a passport photo?
您可以根据我们的营业时间前来。为了避免等待，尤其是在繁忙时段，我们建议您提前预约。You may visit us according to our available operating hours. For a smoother experience, we recommend making a booking in advance, especially during busy periods.
拍 Passport Photo 需要多久？How long does a passport photo session take?
Passport 和 Visa Photo 的拍摄过程一般较快。实际所需时间会根据证件要求以及是否需要其他服务而有所不同。Passport and visa photo sessions are generally quick. The exact time may vary depending on the requirements and whether additional services are needed.
你们有提供 Visa Photo 吗？Do you provide Visa Photos?
有。我们提供不同国家及签证申请所需的 Visa Photo。由于不同国家及签证类型的规格可能不同，建议您提前告诉我们申请的国家和 Visa 类型，我们可以协助确认。Yes. We provide visa photos for different countries and visa applications. Requirements can vary depending on the country and visa type, so please let us know the country and visa type before your session.
拍证件照可以戴眼镜吗？Can I wear glasses for my passport photo?
不同 Passport / Visa 的要求可能有所不同。如果您的申请对眼镜有特别规定，请在拍摄前告诉我们，我们可以根据相关要求协助您。Passport and visa requirements may vary. If your application has specific requirements regarding glasses, please let us know before the session so we can guide you accordingly.
宝宝和小孩可以拍 Passport Photo 吗？Can babies and children take passport photos?
可以。我们提供宝宝及儿童的 Passport / Visa Photo。预约或咨询时，可以告诉我们小孩的年龄，以方便我们提供适当的拍摄安排。Yes. We provide passport and visa photography for children and babies. Please let our team know the age of the child when making your enquiry.
Passport / Visa Photo 可以修图吗？Can passport and visa photos be retouched?
Passport / Visa Photo 主要用于官方身份识别，因此我们不会进行过度修图或改变个人特征的处理，以确保照片保持自然并符合用途。Passport and visa photos need to remain suitable for official identification. We avoid excessive retouching or changes that may affect your natural appearance or identity.
Passport Photo 会不会无法通过 AI / biometric detection？Will my passport photo pass AI or biometric detection?
官方证件照及 biometric system 主要用于身份识别，因此照片需要真实呈现您的外貌。我们会注重灯光、角度、位置及规格，而不是过度修图。Official photo requirements and biometric systems are designed to verify identity, so the photo should accurately represent your natural appearance. We prioritise proper lighting, positioning and compliance with the required specifications rather than excessive editing.
可以额外加印 Passport Photo 吗？Can I order additional passport photo prints?
可以。额外加印可以根据当时提供的选项及最新价格安排。Yes. Additional prints can be arranged according to the available options and current pricing.
🎓 GRADUATION PHOTOGRAPHY

毕业照Graduation Photo

毕业照可以带家人一起拍吗？Can my family join the graduation photoshoot?
可以。家人可以一起参与毕业照拍摄，具体人数及安排根据所选择的配套而定。Yes. Family members can join the graduation photoshoot according to the selected package and session arrangement.
毕业照可以带宠物吗？Can I bring my pet to the graduation photoshoot?
可以。毕业照可以带宠物一起拍摄。建议提前告诉我们，以方便团队做好安排。宠物通常不会计算在拍摄人数内；如宠物造成场地、道具或设备损坏，则可能需要由顾客承担相关费用。Yes, pets are welcome for graduation photoshoots. Please let us know in advance so our team can prepare accordingly. Pets are generally not counted toward the number of people in the package, and the customer may be responsible for any damage caused to the venue, props or equipment by the pet.
你们有提供毕业袍吗？Do you provide graduation gowns?
有。部分毕业摄影配套会提供毕业袍租借服务。不同尺寸及类型的毕业袍供应情况可能有所不同。Yes, graduation gown rental is available for selected graduation photography packages. Gown availability may vary depending on the required size and type. 查看毕业袍款式一览 →
可以自己带毕业袍吗？Can I bring my own graduation gown?
可以。如果您希望使用学校自己的毕业袍或个人毕业袍，可以自行携带。Yes. You may bring your own graduation gown if you prefer to use your school's or personal gown.
有提供小朋友的毕业袍吗？Do you provide graduation gowns for children?
部分儿童毕业袍有提供，具体会根据小朋友的年龄及毕业类型而定。目前幼儿园毕业袍与小学毕业袍的供应安排有所不同，建议预约前先向我们确认。Selected children's graduation gowns are available, depending on the child's age and type of graduation. Kindergarten and primary school gown availability may differ — please confirm with us before booking.
可以拍不同组合的毕业照吗？Can we take different group combinations?
可以。根据配套及拍摄时间，您可以安排个人照、朋友合照、父母合照及全家福等不同组合。Yes. You can arrange different combinations such as individual portraits, friends, parents and family during the session, depending on the package and available time.
毕业照会有多少张照片？How many photos will I receive?
最终照片数量根据所选择的配套而定，具体数量请参考最新配套内容。The number of final photos depends on the selected package. Please refer to the latest package details for the exact inclusions.
可以自己准备服装吗？Can I bring my own outfit?
可以。您可以自行准备喜欢的服装或额外搭配。Yes. You are welcome to bring your own outfit or additional clothing for the photoshoot.
拍毕业照应该穿什么？What should I wear for my graduation photos?
建议选择与毕业袍及整体拍摄风格协调的服装。拍摄全家福或朋友合照时，也可以选择颜色比较协调的搭配。We recommend choosing outfits that complement your graduation gown and your preferred photography style. Neutral or coordinated colours usually work well for group portraits.

全家福Family Portrait

全家福最多可以多少人一起拍？How many people can join a family photoshoot?
人数根据所选择的配套及摄影棚空间而定。如果是大家庭，建议咨询时先告诉我们总人数，我们可以协助安排适合的拍摄方式。The number of people depends on the selected package and studio setup. If you have a larger family, let us know the number of people when enquiring so we can recommend the most suitable arrangement.
小孩会算成人数吗？Are children counted as adults in the package?

同一次拍摄可以拍不同组合吗？Can we take different family group combinations?
可以。根据配套及拍摄时间，可以安排不同组合，例如全家、父母与孩子、夫妻或家庭成员个人照片。Yes. Depending on the package and available shooting time, you may take different combinations such as the whole family, parents with children, couples or individual family members.
全家福可以带宠物吗？Can we bring our pets?
可以。适合的 Family Photography 可以带宠物一起拍摄。建议提前告诉我们，以方便我们做好场地安排。Yes, pets are welcome for suitable family photography sessions. Please let us know in advance so we can prepare the studio accordingly.
可以自己准备服装吗？Can we bring our own outfits?
可以。您可以自行准备服装。拍全家福时，我们建议选择彼此协调的颜色和风格，不一定需要穿完全一样的衣服。Yes. You are welcome to bring your own outfits. For family portraits, we recommend choosing colours and styles that complement one another without looking overly identical.
有提供化妆和发型吗？Do you provide makeup and hairstyling?
化妆及发型服务根据所选择的摄影配套而定，请参考最新配套内容或向我们的团队咨询。Makeup and hairstyling availability depends on the selected photography package. Please check the current package details or enquire with our team.
全家福会有多少张照片？How many photos will we receive?
最终照片数量根据所选择的配套而定，具体数量请参考最新配套内容。The number of final photos depends on the selected package. Please refer to the current package details for the exact number of included photos.
拍全家福应该怎样搭配服装？What should we wear for a family photoshoot?
建议选择颜色协调、简单耐看的服装。避免过于复杂的图案，可以让照片的重点更加集中在人和家人之间的互动。We recommend coordinating your outfits through complementary colours and simple styles. Avoiding overly busy patterns can help keep the focus on the people and relationships in the photograph.
👔 BUSINESS PORTRAIT & PROFESSIONAL HEADSHOT

专业形象照 / 职业头像Professional Portrait

什么是 Business Portrait / Professional Headshot？What is a Business Portrait or Professional Headshot?
Professional Portrait / Headshot 是用于展现个人职业形象的照片，常用于 LinkedIn、公司网站、企业资料、个人品牌及职业用途。A professional portrait is a photograph designed to present you professionally for purposes such as LinkedIn, company profiles, websites, corporate materials, personal branding and professional introductions.
摄影师会指导 Pose 吗？Will the photographer guide my posing?
会。拍摄过程中，摄影师会指导您的姿势、站位及表情。您不需要事先懂得如何摆 Pose。Yes. Our photographer will guide you through posing, positioning and expressions during the session. You do not need to know how to pose beforehand.
是摄影师拍摄还是自拍馆？Is the photoshoot done by a photographer or is it self-service?
我们的 Professional Portrait 是由摄影师进行拍摄，并会在过程中指导您完成拍摄。Our professional portrait sessions are conducted with a photographer who guides you throughout the session.
可以自己带服装吗？Can I bring my own outfit?
可以。您可以自行携带服装。建议根据您希望呈现的职业形象选择适合的服装。Yes. You are welcome to bring your own outfits. We recommend choosing outfits that match the professional image you want to present.
一次可以拍几套服装？How many outfits can I wear?
服装套数根据所选择的配套及拍摄时间而定，具体安排请参考最新配套内容。The number of outfits depends on the selected package and available session time. Please refer to the current package details for the exact arrangement.
可以先看看 PREMO 提供的服装吗？Can I see the outfits available at PREMO?
可以。不同摄影棚及尺寸的服装供应可能有所不同，您可以在拍摄前联系我们确认。Yes. Available outfits may vary by studio and size. You may contact us before your session to check the available options.
有提供化妆和发型吗？Do you provide makeup and hairstyling?
化妆及发型服务根据所选择的配套而定，预约前可以先确认最新配套内容。Makeup and hairstyling availability depends on the selected package. Please check the current package details before booking.
可以看你们的形象照作品吗？Can I see your Business Portrait portfolio?
可以。建议您先查看我们最新的作品集，了解 PREMO 的拍摄风格、灯光及不同类型的形象照效果。Yes. We recommend viewing our latest portfolio to see our photography style, lighting and different portrait approaches.
可以拿全部原图 / 未修图照片吗？Can I receive all the original or unedited photos?
照片交付方式及是否包含原图，会根据所选择的配套而定。预约前可以先确认具体配套内容。Photo delivery and original-photo inclusions depend on the selected package. Please check the package details before booking.
多久可以收到照片？How soon will I receive my photos?
照片交付时间根据拍摄服务及配套而定。如果配套包含快速交付，具体时间会在配套资料中注明。Delivery time depends on the selected service and package. For services with fast delivery, the estimated turnaround time will be stated in the package information.
