关于 Premo Studio

About Premo Studio

Premo Studio 是一间专注护照证件照、形象照、毕业照与全家福摄影的工作室，目前在吉隆坡（Old Klang Road）与新山（Mount Austin）设有分店，累积服务超过 500 位顾客，营运 9 年。Premo Studio specializes in passport photos, headshots, graduation photos and family portraits, with branches in Kuala Lumpur and Johor Bahru — 500+ clients served over 9 years.

我们的故事Our Story

从记录一个瞬间，到记录人生不同阶段From capturing a moment to capturing every chapter of life.

PREMO Studio 相信，每一个值得纪念的时刻，都值得被认真记录。At PREMO Studio, we believe that every moment worth remembering deserves to be captured with care.

一张照片，也许只是人生中的一个瞬间，却可能在多年以后，成为我们重新回到那个时刻的方式。A photograph may only capture a single moment in time, but years later, it can become a way to return to a memory, a person, or a chapter of life.

从毕业、家庭团聚，到个人职业形象，以及日常生活中所需要的证件照片，我们希望用专业摄影，让这些不同人生阶段的瞬间，都能够被好好保存下来。From graduation and family portraits to professional headshots and everyday passport & visa photos, we aim to make photography more meaningful, approachable, and accessible for everyone.

随着 PREMO Studio 不断成长，我们也从单一的摄影服务，逐渐发展出不同类型的摄影体验，包括毕业照、全家福、专业形象照，以及 Passport & Visa Photo。As PREMO Studio continues to grow, so has the way we serve our customers. What started as a photography service has grown into a studio offering Graduation Photography, Family Portraits, Professional Headshots, and Passport & Visa Photography.

今天，PREMO Studio 在 Kuala Lumpur 与 Johor Bahru 提供摄影服务。Today, PREMO Studio serves customers in Kuala Lumpur and Johor Bahru.

无论你是准备迎接人生的新阶段、和家人留下珍贵的合照、为自己的职业形象准备一张照片，还是只是需要一张符合要求的证件照——我们希望你来到 PREMO Studio 时，不只是得到一张照片，而是得到一张值得留下来的照片。Whether you're celebrating a new chapter, gathering your family for a portrait, building your professional image, or simply needing a passport photo — we want you to leave with more than just a photograph. We want you to have something worth keeping.
