About Premo Studio

Premo Studio 是一间专注护照证件照、形象照、毕业照与全家福摄影的工作室，目前在吉隆坡（Old Klang Road）与新山（Mount Austin）设有分店，累积服务超过 500 位顾客，营运 9 年。Premo Studio specializes in passport photos, headshots, graduation photos and family portraits, with branches in Kuala Lumpur and Johor Bahru — 500+ clients served over 9 years.
