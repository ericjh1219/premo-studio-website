实际的差异在哪里？What Are the Real Differences?

最大的差异在于拍摄时长（10 分钟 vs 30 分钟）以及是否包含化妆造型与服装。RM99 的 Express 套餐适合已经打理好、只需要快速拍一张专业照片的情况；RM299 的套餐则多了充足的拍摄时间、专业化妆造型与服装，成品的完整度与精致度会更高。The biggest difference is session length (10 min vs 30 min) and whether makeup/hairstyling and wardrobe are included. RM99 Express suits someone already camera-ready who just needs a quick professional shot. RM299 gives you more time plus professional makeup, hairstyling and an outfit, for a more polished result.

03 · WHEN TO CHOOSE RM299

什么时候该选 RM299？When Should You Choose RM299?

如果这张形象照会长期用在 LinkedIn、公司官网或需要展现专业度的场合，投资 RM299 的套餐会更划算，因为多了化妆造型与服装，成品也更完整；如果只是临时需要一张专业照片、时间有限，RM99 的 Express 套餐已经足够。If this headshot will be used long-term on LinkedIn, your company website, or anywhere professionalism matters, the RM299 package is the better investment — the added makeup, styling and outfit make for a more complete result. For a quick, one-off need, RM99 Express is enough.
