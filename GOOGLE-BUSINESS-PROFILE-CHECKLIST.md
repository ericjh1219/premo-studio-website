# Google Business Profile Checklist — PREMO Studio (KL & JB)

Why this matters: your biggest search demand in Google Search Console is "near me" passport photo searches (for example "passport photo near me", "passport photo shop near me", "kedai gambar passport near me"). Those are mostly decided by the Google Maps / local results, which are controlled by your Google Business Profile, not by the website. This is a to-do list for you; it cannot be done from the website files.

Search Console data referenced (Jul–Oct 2026): "passport photo near me" about 1,575 impressions at average position 6.6 with 2 clicks; "passport photo shop near me" about 1,537 impressions with 6 clicks.

## Do this for each branch (KL Old Klang Road, JB Mount Austin)

1. **One profile per studio.** Each physical studio gets its own profile. Do not use one profile for both.
2. **Name exactly as on the website.** "PREMO Studio" (do not stuff keywords into the name — Google can suspend profiles for that).
3. **Primary category.** Choose the category that matches your main money service for that branch (for KL, check whether "Passport photo studio" or "Photo studio" fits best); add "Portrait studio" and "Graduation photographer"-type secondary categories where Google offers them.
4. **Address, phone and website must match the website exactly.**
   - KL: No 382A, Jalan Seragam, Taman United Garden, 58200 Kuala Lumpur — +6018 793 1888

6. **Services list.** Add Passport photo (from RM49), Visa photo, Business headshot, Graduation photography, Family portrait, with the same limited-time prices shown on the website.
7. **Photos.** Upload real photos of the studio front, signage, inside, the shooting area, and sample results. Add new photos regularly.
8. **Booking link.** Use `https://premostudio.minibookit.com/` as the appointment link.
9. **Questions & answers.** Seed the Q&A with the same answers as the website FAQ (price, how long, Thursday closure, glasses).
10. **Posts.** Post an offer or update every couple of weeks.

## Reviews (the largest ranking lever you control)

- Ask every happy passport and graduation customer for a Google review, with a QR code or short link at the counter and in the WhatsApp message after the shoot.
- Reply to every review, positive or negative, within a few days.
- Do not offer rewards for reviews and do not ask for only positive ones — both break Google's rules.
- Keep the website's review count claim ("5.0★, 130+ Google reviews") in line with what Google actually shows.

## Keep these consistent everywhere (NAP: name, address, phone)

Facebook, Instagram bio, the website footer and any directory listing should show the same name, address and phone number as the profile.

Review Google Business Profile Insights (searches, calls, direction requests) and compare with Search Console clicks on the passport pages.
