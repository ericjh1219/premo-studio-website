02 · WHERE YOU WILL USE IT

形象照会用在哪里？Where You Will Actually Use It

LinkedIn 与求职平台LinkedIn & Job Platforms
个人头像是别人看到你的第一样东西，清晰、光线均匀的正面照更容易建立信任。Your profile photo is the first thing people see; a clear, evenly lit front-facing shot builds trust.

同一套背景与光线的团队照，让整个品牌看起来统一专业。Team photos shot with the same background and lighting make the whole brand look consistent.

如果只是护照或证件用途，不需要形象照，请看 护照照片。If you only need a passport or ID photo, you don't need a headshot — see passport photos.
03 · HOW TO TELL IF YOU NEED ONE

怎么判断自己需要不需要How to Tell Whether You Need One

问自己三个问题：别人会不会在网上先看到你的照片？你现在的照片是不是几年前的、或是随手拍的？你的工作是否靠信任成交？只要有一个答案是“是”，专业形象照通常就值得。Ask yourself three questions: will people see your photo online before meeting you? Is your current photo years old or a casual snapshot? Does your work depend on trust? If any answer is yes, a professional headshot is usually worth it.
