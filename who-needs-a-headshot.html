FAQ

拍摄需要多久？How long does a headshot session take?
一般约 30–60 分钟，依套餐而定。Typically 30–60 minutes, depending on the package.
需要化妆吗？Do I need makeup?
建议做基础造型或补妆，让镜头前的状态更好；男生也适用，妆感会非常自然、不明显。Light grooming/makeup is recommended for the best on-camera results — this applies to men too, and it looks natural, not obvious.
多久可以拿到照片？When will I receive my photos?
精修照片会在短时间内交付，实际时间以预约时门店说明为准。Final images are delivered within a short turnaround time.
拍摄当天可以选片吗？Can I choose the photos on the same day?
可以，拍摄结束后可以现场初选喜欢的照片，方便安排后续精修。Yes — you can do an initial selection on the spot right after the shoot.
