毕业照拍摄前准备清单The Complete Graduation Photo Preparation Checklist

做好这些准备，让拍摄当天更从容，也能拍出你想要的毕业照。A little planning goes a long way toward a relaxed session and photos you'll love.

更新于 2026年9月 Updated: September 2026 ｜ 阅读时间 Reading time: 5 min
01 · OUTFIT & GOWN

服装与毕业袍Outfit & Gown

现场备有超过 15 所大学 / 学院的毕业袍可免费借用，已包含在毕业照套餐内，也欢迎自备喜欢的服装或额外搭配。想指定特定披肩颜色或款式？每种颜色我们只有一件，建议预约时先告知想要的款式代号，以便提前准备。Real gowns from 15+ universities and colleges are free to borrow and included in every graduation photo package — you're also welcome to bring your own outfit. Want a specific shawl color or style? We only keep one of each, so let us know your preferred style code when booking.

02 · CERTIFICATE & PROPS

证书与道具Certificate & Props

如果学校已经颁发证书或证书筒，欢迎带来拍摄——证书 / 学位照是很受欢迎的组合。还没拿到证书也没关系，学士服写真、流苏与学位帽同样能拍出仪式感。If your school has already issued your diploma or scroll, feel free to bring it along — certificate photos are a popular combination. Don't have it yet? Your gown, tassel and mortarboard alone carry plenty of ceremony.

04 · TIMING & BOOKING

拍摄时长与预约时间Duration & Booking Timing

个人独照约 20–30 分钟，团体照与全家福依人数调整。毕业季（约 5–8 月及 11–1 月，视各校而定）是拍摄旺季，建议提前预约以确保有理想时段；周末照常营业（除星期四公休）。Individual portraits take about 20–30 minutes; group and family sessions take longer depending on numbers. Graduation season (roughly May–August and November–January, depending on the university) is peak — book ahead. We're open weekends too, except Thursdays.

05 · ON THE DAY

拍摄当天On Shoot Day

不需要会摆姿势——摄影师会全程指导姿势、位置与表情，让整个拍摄过程轻松自然。可以选择影棚拍摄或校园户外拍摄，拍摄后即可预览照片。No posing experience needed — your photographer guides positioning and expressions throughout. Choose a studio session or campus outdoor shoot, with instant preview right after shooting.
