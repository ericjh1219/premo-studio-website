毕业照穿搭指南What to Wear for Your Graduation Photo

选对服装，让毕业照拍出最好看的自己。The right outfit choices help your graduation photos look their best.

更新于 2026年5月 Updated: May 2026 ｜ 阅读时间 Reading time: 5 min
示意图，非实际拍摄成果 · Illustrative image, not an actual Premo Studio shoot result
01 · GENERAL TIPS

整体穿搭原则General Tips

以简洁、合身为主，避免过于花俏的图案，颜色尽量与毕业袍或背景相衬，让焦点回到你的表情与笑容上。Keep it simple and well-fitted, avoid overly busy patterns, and choose colors that complement your gown or backdrop so the focus stays on your expression and smile.

02 · MALE & FEMALE RECOMMENDATIONS

男生女生穿搭建议Male & Female Recommendations

推荐 RECOMMENDED
男生：合身衬衫／polo衫，素色为佳 Men: fitted shirt or polo, solid colors
女生：合身上衣或洋装，简约配饰 Women: fitted top or dress, minimal accessories
颜色与毕业袍协调 Colors that complement the gown
尽量避免 AVOID
过大或过小的尺寸 Ill-fitting sizes (too big or too small)
太花俏的图案或荧光色 Busy prints or neon colors
过多配饰分散焦点 Too many accessories that distract
03 · FOOTWEAR, HAIR & GROOMING

鞋子、发型与仪容Footwear, Hair & Grooming

鞋子Footwear
简约、干净的款式最保险，避免过于抢眼Simple, clean styles are safest — avoid anything too flashy
发型Hair
整理整齐，确保学士帽能稳固戴上Keep it neat and ensure your mortarboard sits securely
仪容Grooming
拍摄前修剪指甲、检查衣领与配件Trim nails and check your collar and accessories beforehand
配饰Accessories
简约为主，突出脸部而非配件Keep it minimal so the focus stays on your face, not accessories
04 · QUICK CHECKLIST

拍摄前快速检查清单Quick Checklist

服装是否合身、颜色是否协调、鞋子与发型是否整齐、配饰是否精简 — 出发拍摄前花几分钟检查一遍，让整体造型更完美。Fit, color coordination, tidy shoes and hair, minimal accessories — take a few minutes to check these before heading to your shoot for a polished look.

FAQ

毕业照拍摄需要多久？How long does a graduation photoshoot take?
一般独照约 20–30 分钟，团体照依人数调整，实际时长请以预约时门店说明为准。Solo shots take about 20–30 minutes; group shoots vary by size.
可以加拍家人合照吗？Can I include family photos?
可以，建议预约时先告知，方便安排足够的拍摄时间与场地。Yes — just let us know when booking so we can plan enough time.
多久可以拿到照片？When will I receive my photos?
依套餐而定：基础套餐约 1–2 周，标准套餐约 3–5 天，进阶套餐约 2–3 天，实际时间请以预约时门店说明为准。Basic ~1–2 weeks, Standard ~3–5 days, Premium ~2–3 days.
户外拍摄遇到下雨可以改期吗？Can I reschedule if it rains?
可以，户外拍摄若遇天气不佳，欢迎与门店联系改期，也可以选择改在影棚拍摄。Yes — contact us to reschedule, or switch to a studio session.
