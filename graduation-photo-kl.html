Graduation Photography FAQ

摄影师会指导姿势吗？Do I need to know how to pose?
不需要。摄影师会全程指导姿势、位置与表情，轻松自然地完成拍摄。No. The photographer guides posing, positioning and expressions throughout the session.
可以约同学一起拍团体照吗？Can we book a group shoot with classmates?
可以，团拍前建议先告知人数，方便安排场地与拍摄时间，也可以同时安排每人的独照。Yes — let us know the group size when booking so we can plan the space and time.
家人可以一起拍吗？Can my family join the graduation photos?
可以，家人可以一起参与拍摄，具体人数及安排根据所选择的配套而定。Yes — family members can join according to the selected package and session arrangement.
可以带宠物一起拍吗？Can I bring my pet?
可以，建议提前告诉我们以方便安排；宠物通常不计入拍摄人数。Yes — just let us know in advance. Pets are generally not counted toward the package's headcount.
你们有提供毕业袍吗？Do you provide graduation gowns?
有，现场备有超过 15 所大学 / 学院的毕业袍可免费借用；也欢迎自备学士服。Yes — real gowns from 15+ universities are free to borrow, or bring your own.
可以自己带服装吗？Can I bring my own outfit?
可以，欢迎自行准备喜欢的服装或额外搭配。Yes — you're welcome to bring your own outfit or additional clothing.
多久可以拿到照片？When will I receive my photos?
预览照片一般在拍摄后短时间内提供，精修完成的正式照片交付时间依套餐而定，请以预约时门店说明为准。Preview photos are available shortly after the shoot; final delivery time depends on the package.
可以周末预约吗？Can I book on weekends?
可以，除了星期四公休，其余时间（包含周末）都正常营业。Yes — we're open every day except Thursdays, including weekends.
毕业季会不会很难订到时段？Is it hard to book during graduation season?
毕业季（约 5–8 月及 11–1 月，视各校而定）是拍摄旺季，建议提前预约以确保有理想时段。Graduation season is peak — book ahead to secure your preferred slot.
